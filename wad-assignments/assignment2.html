HTML Listing:

These tags are used to build lists in HTML. They organize related items into either bulleted or numbered structures, which makes content highly readable and accessible.

  • Unordered List (ul): Creates a bulleted list.
  • Ordered List (ol): Creates a numbered list.
  • List Item (li): Represents an individual item within a list.

Modern tags / Equivalents (Semantic Wrappers) for ul, ol, li are semantic layout containers that you must wrap around your lists to give them structural meaning to search engines and screen readers.

These semantic tags are

  1. nav: Represents a section of navigation links.
  2. section: Defines a thematic grouping of content, often with a heading.
  3. article: Represents a self-contained piece of content that could be distributed independently.
  4. aside: Contains content that is tangentially related to the main content, like sidebars or callouts.
  5. header: Represents introductory content or navigational aids for its nearest ancestor sectioning content.
  6. footer: Represents the footer for its nearest ancestor sectioning content, often containing metadata or related links.
If your list consists of website links to navigate your site, wrap it in a <nav> block.
